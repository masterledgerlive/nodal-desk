🧭 BUOY ROUTE — HOW FAR DOWN THE ROUTE IS THIS PLAY?
Buoys are laid along each family's route in our own snapshot space. A forming play crosses them in sequence — hit, hit, hit — each one confirming direction and timing, so the route progress reads BEFORE the big move. The chain's aim is the fire point at the low: the decisive buoy tripping, not the move completing.

Families are computed from the recorded path alone: ONE-TURNER = quiet (range ≤ 0.30) then one move ≥ 0.30 that never crosses back · FLIP = a side led ≥ 0.60 before 240s and lost · CHOP = ≥ 4 crosses of 50¢ · CLEAN = ≤ 1 cross and one side held ≥ 80% of the window · MIXED = everything else. Small families print small numbers — that is the honest confidence.

🌐 OUTWARD TREES — FOLLOW THE LEADER

Money conservation is the axiom: money comes from somewhere and goes somewhere, so the signal shows first somewhere. These pairs are graded on counted market history — correlation scan at every lag, then event-by-event: when the leader moved, did BTC do what the pair says, after the measured lag? An unproven leader is a CANDIDATE — it never fires a live flag. Only a PROVEN pair (≥20 events, hit rate ≥ base + 10pts, |corr| ≥ 0.15) may flag, and its flag carries the lag + hit count.
