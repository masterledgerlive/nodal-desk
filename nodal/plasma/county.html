📍 PINELLAS COUNTY: THE PLAN

National numbers are easy to scroll past. This is the same fight, on our own streets — Clearwater, Largo, St. Petersburg, the whole Tampa Bay shore.

🏠 THE LOCAL PICTURE

The collection centers across Tampa Bay and Pinellas County are run by the same foreign majors named on the money page — CSL (Australia), Grifols (Spain), Takeda's BioLife (Japan), Octapharma (Switzerland), Kedrion (Italy, Permira + ADIA). The plasma leaves Pinellas arms, the drugs are priced like pharmaceuticals, the profits land overseas — and the donors here get the same flat fee and the same free data harvest as everywhere else.

America supplies ~70% of the world's plasma. Pinellas County is part of how. The question this page asks is simple: what if Pinellas donors stopped being a harvest and started being a market?

🪜 THE PLAN, IN STEPS

STEP 1 — THE ROSTER. Build the roster of Pinellas and Tampa Bay donors who say yes — counted, by name, in the open. Every signup on this page is one more name on that roster. A roster is leverage you can point to.
STEP 2 — THE DATA COLLECTIVE. Stand up the data collective under the Data Broker doctrine: donor data default-locked, moving only at the owner's price, bargained as one bloc. The file they keep on you becomes the file you hold.
STEP 3 — COMMUNITY-OWNED COLLECTION. Build toward collection that Pinellas owns — plasma treated as a commodity the community sells, with donors in the value loop, instead of a "donation" the majors buy cheap and a data file they take free.
STEP 4 — THE FREE HARVEST ENDS HERE. When the roster is real and the collective bargains as one, the majors lose their free harvest in Pinellas: no more flat-fee-only plasma, no more unpaid data, no more all-the-risk-on-the-donor weeks. What they want from this county gets negotiated — with this county.

🎁 WHAT MEMBERS GET

  • A count that counts. Your name on the roster is bargaining power — for you and for every donor standing next to you.
  • Your data, locked by default. Under the doctrine, your information moves only at your price and on your say — and the collective negotiates that price as one.
  • A seat in the value loop. As community-owned collection is built, members are the owners — the findings, and the value, stay community property.
  • The receipts, first. Members hear what the research finds — the money, the map, the rules — as it lands, in plain language, on pages like these.

🌎 AND IT'S ALREADY MOVING ELSEWHERE

Canada, 2026 (REPORTED): after two donor deaths in Winnipeg — which Health Canada found were not linked to plasma collection — regulators partially suspended one centre's licence over compliance findings, Grifols shut all 17 of its Canadian centres in mid-August 2026, and reopening began Oct 20–21, 2026 under a plan coordinated with Health Canada and Canadian Blood Services. An organized campaign (the Canadian Health Coalition and NUPGE) is now calling for a total ban on for-profit paid plasma. The framing is being challenged in public, right now — Pinellas doesn't have to go first to go.
