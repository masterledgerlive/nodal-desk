💰 FOLLOW THE MONEY

The companies, named. Who collects America's plasma, where they're run from, and what they report making — from their own filings. Figures are REPORTED from company filings unless marked ESTIMATED.

🏢 THE MAJORS — EVERY ONE FOREIGN-RUN

CSL Limited — Melbourne, Australia 🇦🇺

Runs CSL Plasma (~320 US centers, US HQ Boca Raton, FL; ~17,000 staff in 2023). FY2024/25 (ended Jun 2025, USD): revenue $15,558M (+5%) · gross margin 54.3% · EBITDA $5,151M · net profit $3,136M. CSL Behring (the plasma arm): revenue $11,158M — immunoglobulin $6,064M, albumin $1,297M, haemophilia $1,488M. The company states collection costs "including donor compensation and labour" are "continuing to reduce." CEO pay: $6.06M.

Grifols S.A. — Barcelona, Spain 🇪🇸

Runs Grifols Plasma / Biomat USA (~150+ US centers). FY2025 (EUR): revenue €7,524M (+7%) · gross margin 38.0% · adj. EBITDA €1,825M · net profit €402M (more than doubled) · leverage 4.2×. Immunoglobulin = 54.3% of group revenue. Cites "continued cost per liter reduction." In its own words (Jul 2026): "approximately 25% of plasma collected in the United States is used to supply international demand."

Takeda — Tokyo, Japan 🇯🇵

Runs BioLife (acquired via Shire, 2019). FY2025 (ended Mar 2026, JPY): revenue ¥4,505.7B · gross margin 65.1% · core operating profit ¥1,172.5B. Plasma-derived therapies ≈ ¥930.9B (~21% of sales): Ig ¥790.6B, albumin ¥140.3B. A ¥403.5B litigation provision flipped reported results to a net loss of ¥152.4B. CEO pay ¥2,315M (~$14.6M).

Octapharma AG — Lachen, Switzerland 🇨🇭

Runs Octapharma Plasma (~190 US centers, 35 states). Family-owned. FY2025 (EUR): net sales €3.64B (+5%) · operating income €577M (15.9% margin). No gross margin or segment disclosure — what they don't publish, we don't invent.

Kedrion — Italy 🇮🇹 · owned by Permira + ADIA (Abu Dhabi)

Runs KEDPLASMA (~76 US centers after combining with BPL). Taken private in Jan 2022 by the private-equity firm Permira and ADIA, Abu Dhabi's sovereign wealth fund. FY2025 (EUR): revenue €1.65B (+4.5%) · adj. EBITDA €367M (~22.2% margin). The US = 61% of its sales.

THE HONEST EXCEPTION: ADMA Biologics (Ramsey, NJ; fractionation in Boca Raton, FL) is genuinely American — revenue $510.2M (+20%), gross margin 57.4%, adj. EBITDA $231M (45.3%, the highest margin in the set), net profit $146.9M. It is small next to the giants. The foreign-ownership claim is about the majors — and we say "the majors" every time, never "all."

🧬 WHAT A LITER BECOMES — ESTIMATED

No company publishes an absolute dollar-per-liter figure — that number is not disclosed anywhere. What can be built, labelled ESTIMATED:

  • ~4.5 g of IVIG per liter × ~$73.89/g ≈ $333 from IgG alone, plus albumin (~25.7 g/L) and other fractions.
  • Industry-site estimate: $150–$300 wholesale per donation (~$170–$430 per liter).
  • Payout-to-value ratio ≈ 15–40% of per-donation wholesale value (ESTIMATED) — the donor's flat fee is a small fraction of what that donation becomes.
  • On the shelf: IVIG ~$73.89/gram (ESTIMATED, market research) · a full course ~$10,000 · a year of therapy >$30,000 · albumin vials $53–$131 cash (REPORTED, Drugs.com, Oct 2026).

Donor pay, for comparison: $30–$70/session typical; $400+/month with bonuses; up to ~$1,000/month high-frequency; one documented donor made >$8,000/year at ~$77/session (CNN).

🗓️ THE TAKEOVER TIMELINE — HOW FIVE MAJORS ATE THE MAP

2009 — The FTC blocks CSL's $3.1B bid for Talecris. The government already sees plasma as strategic.
2011 — Grifols buys Talecris for ~$4B. Three big players left standing.
2015 — Baxter spins off Baxalta.
2016 — Shire buys Baxalta for $32B. China's Creat Group buys BPL (UK) for $1.02B.
2017 — Creat's €1.3B bid for Biotest is aborted under CFIUS national-security scrutiny — the precedent: Washington has already treated plasma assets as security-sensitive.
2019 — Takeda buys Shire for $62B — BioLife becomes Japanese-run.
2022 — Permira + ADIA combine Kedrion + BPL (€1.1B revenue). Grifols completes its Biotest acquisition (€1.6B equity / €2B enterprise value).

WHAT WE COULD NOT VERIFY — LEFT OUT, NOT FILLED IN: absolute cost per liter of source plasma; donor compensation as a share of cost of goods sold; CSL Behring's segment EBIT; Octapharma's gross margin and net profit; Kedrion's full-year net profit; center-level pay at individual chains. No company discloses these. Anyone who quotes you an exact cost-per-liter is guessing.
