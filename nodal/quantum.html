#8 D-Wave · Quantum annealing

🖥️ Machines: Advantage — 5,000+ annealing qubits (Pegasus topology) · Advantage2 — 4,400+ annealing qubits (Zephyr topology, general availability 2025-05-20).

⚛️ The physics: Quantum annealing with superconducting flux qubits — NOT a gate-based computer. The system is slowly evolved toward its lowest-energy state, which encodes the answer to an optimisation problem written as an Ising model or QUBO.

⚖️ What it does vs the others: Good at: large combinatorial optimisation and sampling (scheduling, routing, portfolio-style QUBOs) and quantum simulation of magnetic materials, with hybrid classical-quantum solvers for oversized problems.
