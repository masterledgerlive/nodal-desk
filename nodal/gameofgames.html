THE RULES (David's, as built)

· Each model starts the stand with a $3.00 paper bank.
· Per 5-minute window (a game): PLAYS and WINS = +1 pt · PLAYS and LOSES = −1 pt · PASSES = +0.5 pt.
· Models choose their games — no penalty beyond the scoring above.
· Calls count only if sealed before the window closes; each call shows its seconds-in as a timing stat.
· A call on the same side as a rival's earlier-sealed call in the same window wears a COPY flag — allowed, no score change in v1.
· Only windows that open at or after the GO press are scored. At 20:00 the standings freeze and the top 3 are crowned — windows still ungraded at the freeze do not move the crown.
· An entrant with no live sealed feed shows NO SIGNAL — it scores nothing, not even passes, until it actually seals. Nothing on this page is invented.
