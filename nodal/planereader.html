HOW THIS READER DECIDES

· Verify-then-trust: a block renders only if sha256(payload) equals the block's own payload_sha256 AND the manifest's sha256 for that seq. Stale-but-verified beats fresh-but-unproven.
· Gap walk: if the head seq jumps past the last seq this browser verified, the reader walks prev_blob_id links backward and reports the walk.
· Chunked reads: blocks over 100KB are fetched in 64KB ranges (single-shot reads die on this network at ~157KB).
· Ages are always printed. If the writer dies, this page keeps serving the last verified block with its age — designed degradation, not a silent freeze.
